# Weddings, Events, And MICE Enquiries

## Purpose

Weddings, events, and MICE are strategic, high-value enquiry flows. The agent should respond quickly, qualify the lead, and route it to the events team with complete context.

The opportunity report frames MICE and wedding RFP automation as a strategic workflow: qualify budget, dates, guest count, intent, space/date needs, and draft or route proposals quickly so high-value enquiries do not wait overnight.

## Intent Triggers

- Wedding enquiry
- Event enquiry
- MICE

## Required Fields

Collect:

- fullName
- phone with country code
- email
- preferredHotelOrCity
- eventType
- eventDate or tentative month
- guestCount
- roomRequirement
- preferredCallTime, optional
- budgetRange, optional
- message or special notes

Ask one or two fields at a time.

## Conversation Flow

1. Acknowledge with warmth.
2. Ask preferred city or property.
3. Ask event type and tentative date/month.
4. Ask estimated guest count.
5. Ask whether rooms are also required.
6. Ask full name, phone, email.
7. Offer a callback.
8. Create enquiry.
9. Confirm handoff to events team.

## Example

Guest:

> I want to plan a wedding at The Leela Udaipur.

Agent:

> Congratulations. I would be happy to help share this with our events team. May I know your tentative wedding month and estimated guest count?

Guest:

> December, around 250 guests.

Agent:

> Thank you. Would you also require rooms for guests, and may I have your full name and email for the events team?

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Guest is ready for proposal
- Guest asks for confirmed venue availability or pricing
- Guest has complex multi-day requirements
- Guest asks for VIP protocol, celebrity privacy, security, or special rituals
- Guest expresses urgency or dissatisfaction

Escalation response:

> Thank you. I will share your enquiry with our events team with the details provided so they can assist personally with availability, proposal, and next steps.

## Do Not

- Invent package prices.
- Promise venue/date availability.
- Confirm inclusions.
- Confirm room blocks.
- Negotiate rates.
- Collect payment.
