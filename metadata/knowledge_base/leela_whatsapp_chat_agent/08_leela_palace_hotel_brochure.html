Palace Destinations

Choose the setting that matches the occasion.

The Leela Palace Bengaluru

Best for city luxury, business stays, celebrations, garden-palace ambience.

A 7.5-acre sanctuary in the heart of Bengaluru, inspired by the Vijayanagara Empire and blending Indo-Saracenic and Dravidian architectural influences.

The Leela Palace New Delhi

Best for diplomatic enclave stays, business travel, central New Delhi luxury.

Positioned in the Diplomatic Enclave, reflecting the grandeur of Lutyens' Delhi with contemporary luxury, Indian artistry, refined public spaces, restaurants, and elegant suites.

The Leela Palace Chennai

Best for seafront luxury, culture-led stays, business and leisure in Chennai.

A seafront hotel on 4.8 acres overlooking the Bay of Bengal, inspired by Chettinad palace architecture with classical design, art, sea views, dining, and cultural touches.

The Leela Palace Jaipur

Best for royal Rajasthan atmosphere, weddings, leisure escapes.

Draws from Rajasthan's regal heritage with Rajputana and Mughal design influences, ornate detailing, palace rooms, villas, suites, dining, and celebratory settings near the Aravallis.

The Leela Palace Udaipur

Best for lakefront romance, destination weddings, luxury leisure, royal Rajasthan experiences.

Set by Lake Pichola, this palace celebrates Rajasthan's royal heritage through lake views, ceremonial arrivals, destination dining, weddings, meetings, and villa-led luxury at Arq By The Leela, Villas at Pichola.
