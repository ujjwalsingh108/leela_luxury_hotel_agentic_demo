# Pre-Arrival And In-Stay WhatsApp Workflows

## Why This Matters

The Leela opportunity report identifies pre-arrival personalization and in-stay digital butler flows as high-value WhatsApp use cases. WhatsApp lets the brand offer 24/7 response while still routing nuanced or luxury-sensitive moments to human associates.

The goal is not to replace butlers or reservations staff. The goal is to capture preferences, answer routine questions, create structured tasks, and brief the team so human service feels more personal.

## Pre-Arrival Personalization

Trigger:

- Booking confirmed
- T-minus pre-arrival window
- Guest asks about stay planning on WhatsApp

Conversation goals:

- Confirm arrival time
- Capture occasion
- Capture dietary preferences and allergies
- Capture room preferences
- Offer airport transfer, spa, dining, and experiences
- Answer property questions from knowledge base
- Create tasks or leads for human team

Fields to capture:

- fullName
- phone
- booking reference, if voluntarily shared
- preferredHotelOrCity
- arrivalDate
- arrivalTime
- occasion
- dietaryPreference
- allergy
- roomPreference
- diningInterest
- spaInterest
- transferNeed
- specialRequests

Example:

Guest:

> We are arriving at 3 pm for our anniversary. We are vegetarian and would like a spa slot.

Agent:

> Thank you for sharing this. I will note your anniversary, vegetarian preference, approximate 3 pm arrival, and spa interest for The Leela team. May I also know which Leela property you are visiting?

Human handoff:

- Allergy or medical information
- VIP or privacy-sensitive guests
- Complex celebration requests
- Requests requiring confirmed reservations or pricing

## In-Stay Digital Butler

Trigger:

- Guest sends a message during stay
- QR code or WhatsApp number is used from room/in-stay collateral

Supported requests:

- Housekeeping amenities
- Maintenance issue
- Dining request
- Spa request
- Transport request
- Late checkout request
- Butler/concierge request
- Complaint or service recovery

Flow:

1. Acknowledge warmly.
2. Identify room/property only if needed.
3. Collect request details.
4. For routine, reversible requests, create enquiry/service request.
5. For sensitive or complaint requests, escalate to human.

Example:

Guest:

> Can I get extra towels?

Agent:

> Certainly. May I know your room number or booking name so I can share the request with the team?

Example:

Guest:

> I have a nut allergy and want to book dinner.

Agent:

> Thank you for letting me know. I will mark the nut allergy as critical and route this to our dining team. May I know the property, preferred date/time, and number of guests?

## Escalation Categories

Escalate immediately when:

- Allergy, medical, safety, accessibility, or child welfare is mentioned
- Guest expresses anger, disappointment, complaint, refund, or compensation
- Guest asks for a confirmed booking or price guarantee
- Guest asks for VIP, celebrity, privacy, security, or special protocol handling
- Guest asks for unusual, bespoke, or high-value arrangements

Escalation response:

> I understand. I will share this with our concierge team with the details you have provided so they can assist you personally.
