## Brochure Body

### Welcome To The Leela Palace Hotels

The Leela Palaces, Hotels and Resorts is known for true Indian luxury, warm hospitality, rich cultural settings, elegant architecture, art, cuisine, and carefully curated guest experiences. Its palace hotels are designed for guests seeking a stay that feels gracious, personal, and deeply connected to place.

The Leela experience is guided by the Indian hospitality philosophy of Atithi Devo Bhava, placing the guest at the heart of the journey. Across the palace hotels, guests can expect refined rooms and suites, destination-led dining, wellness experiences, celebrations, and attentive service delivered with discretion.

### Palace Destinations

#### The Leela Palace Bengaluru

Best for: city luxury, business stays, celebrations, garden-palace ambience.

The Leela Palace Bengaluru is a 7.5-acre sanctuary in the heart of Bengaluru, inspired by the Vijayanagara Empire and blending Indo-Saracenic and Dravidian architectural influences. It offers access to the city's business and leisure areas while preserving a calm palace-style retreat.

Typical guest interests:

- Business and leisure stays in Bengaluru
- Regal rooms and suites
- Dining, meetings, celebrations, and city access
- Guests seeking a classic palace hotel in Bengaluru

#### The Leela Palace New Delhi

Best for: diplomatic enclave stays, business travel, central New Delhi luxury, refined city experiences.

The Leela Palace New Delhi is positioned in the heart of the Diplomatic Enclave and reflects the grandeur of Lutyens' Delhi with contemporary luxury, Indian artistry, refined public spaces, restaurants, and elegant suites.

Typical guest interests:

- Business, diplomatic, and leisure stays in New Delhi
- Central access to historic landmarks and business districts
- Premium suites, dining, wellness, and celebrations
- Guests seeking classic city luxury in the capital

#### The Leela Palace Chennai

Best for: seafront luxury, culture-led stays, business and leisure in Chennai.

The Leela Palace Chennai is a seafront hotel on 4.8 acres overlooking the Bay of Bengal. Inspired by Chettinad palace architecture, it combines classical design, art, sea views, dining, and cultural touches.

Typical guest interests:

- Seafront luxury in Chennai
- Business travel, family stays, and special occasions
- Dining, wellness, and elegant rooms or suites
- Guests who value coastal views and cultural character

#### The Leela Palace Jaipur

Best for: royal Rajasthan atmosphere, weddings, leisure escapes, palace-style celebrations.

The Leela Palace Jaipur draws from Rajasthan's regal heritage with Rajputana and Mughal design influences, ornate detailing, palace rooms, villas, suites, dining, and celebratory settings near the Aravallis.

Typical guest interests:

- Romantic and leisure stays near Jaipur
- Destination weddings and celebrations
- Palace rooms, villas, suites, and curated dining
- Guests seeking Rajasthan grandeur and resort-like calm

#### The Leela Palace Udaipur

Best for: lakefront romance, destination weddings, luxury leisure, royal Rajasthan experiences.

The Leela Palace Udaipur sits by Lake Pichola and celebrates Rajasthan's royal heritage through lake views, palace architecture, ceremonial arrivals, refined rooms and suites, destination dining, weddings, and meetings. The property also references Arq By The Leela, Villas at Pichola for an ultra-luxury villa experience.

Typical guest interests:

- Romantic lakefront stays
- Destination weddings and celebrations
- Palace rooms, suites, and villa-led luxury
- Dining, meetings, and curated Udaipur experiences
