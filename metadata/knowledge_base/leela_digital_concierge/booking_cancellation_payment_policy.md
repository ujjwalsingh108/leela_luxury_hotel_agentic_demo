# Booking, Cancellation, Payment, and Guest Policy

Document status: Production-ready public-facing knowledge base draft  
Audience: Website visitors, booking prospects, reservations leads  
Last reviewed: 2026-06-29  
Primary public sources:
- https://www.theleela.com/general-terms-and-conditions

## Purpose

Use this document when guests ask about booking steps, cancellation, no-show, late cancellation, payment guarantee, taxes, ID requirements, food and beverage policy, allergens, privacy, official channels, and reservation safety.

This document is for public guidance only. The final applicable policy is the policy shown during booking, the selected offer terms, and the confirmation issued by the hotel or booking engine.

## Core Booking Rule

The concierge can guide booking and create a lead, but cannot complete payment or guarantee reservation unless integrated with an approved booking system that returns an official confirmation.

Safe response:

"I can guide you through the booking enquiry and direct you to the official reservations page. Final rates, taxes, cancellation policy, and confirmation will be shown during the official booking process."

## Official Booking Channel

Use:

https://reservations.theleela.com

Official website:

https://www.theleela.com

Agent must not:

- Ask for card numbers in chat.
- Send payment links unless generated by approved backend.
- Validate unknown payment links.
- Ask guests to pay through personal phone numbers or unofficial accounts.

Optional:

## Booking Confirmation Rule

The agent may say:

- "I have prepared your booking enquiry."
- "Please continue to the official reservations page."
- "The reservations team can confirm availability."

The agent must not say:

- "Your booking is confirmed."
- "Your room is held."
- "The rate is guaranteed."
- "You have been upgraded."

Unless an official booking API returns:

- Confirmation number
- Confirmed room type
- Rate
- Taxes/fees
- Cancellation terms
- Payment status

## Cancellation and No-Show Guidance

The official terms state that no-show and late cancellation charges may apply according to booking or offer policy. Some reservations must be guaranteed by credit card, and the hotel may charge no-show or late cancellation retention plus applicable taxes as per defined policy.

Agent response:

"Cancellation and no-show terms depend on the selected rate, offer, dates, and property. The official booking page or reservation confirmation will show the applicable policy before confirmation."

If guest asks:

"What is the cancellation policy?"

Answer:

"It varies by rate and offer. Some bookings may have late cancellation or no-show charges. Please check the policy shown during booking or share your confirmation details with reservations for exact terms."

## Payment and Credit Card Guidance

The official terms indicate that reservations may need to be guaranteed by credit card, and for pay-at-hotel bookings the card may be used to guarantee the stay. Final bill authorization and no-show or late cancellation charges may apply according to policy.

Agent must:

- Direct payment/card entry to official secure booking page.
- Never collect card number, CVV, OTP, or banking details in chat.
- Escalate suspicious payment questions.

Safe response:

"For your security, please enter payment or card guarantee details only on the official reservations page or through verified hotel channels. I cannot collect card details in chat."

## Taxes, Fees, and Charges

Guest is responsible for charges, fees, duties, taxes, and assessments arising from use of the website or booking. The exact taxes and fees should be confirmed during booking.

Agent guidance:

- Say rates may be subject to taxes and fees.
- Do not calculate taxes unless live booking API provides them.

## Identification and Arrival

The official terms state that guests are required to produce original proof of identity at arrival.

Agent response:

"Guests are generally required to present valid original identification at check-in. The property team can advise if you have a specific ID or documentation question."

## Reservation Transfer and Name Change

The official terms state that reservations are not transferable and name changes are not allowed.

Agent response:

"Name changes or transfers may not be permitted. Please contact reservations directly with your booking details so they can advise based on your confirmed reservation."

## Conduct, Safety, and Security

The hotel reserves rights related to guest conduct, safety, security, and medical reasons. Guests must not compromise health, safety, or security at the hotel.

Agent guidance:

- Escalate safety, medical, legal, harassment, or emergency issues.
- Do not debate hotel policy.
- Offer contact with hotel team.

## Outside Food and Beverage

Official terms indicate guests are not allowed to bring food and beverage from outside into the hotel or order outside food to the hotel.

Agent response:

"Outside food and beverage may not be permitted under hotel policy. If you have a dietary need, I can share it with the hotel team for guidance."

## Allergies and Dietary Restrictions

Official terms warn that hotel food and beverage operations may involve potential allergens and that guests with allergies, sensitivities, or intolerances should advise hotel management before arrival.

Agent must:

- Capture allergy details.
- Mark as sensitive.
- Route to property team.
- Avoid guarantees.

Safe response:

"I will note the allergy carefully, but the hotel team should confirm what can be safely arranged. May I know the specific allergy and the property/date of your visit?"

## Valuables and Lost Property

Official terms reference in-room safety deposit boxes and hotel handling of valuables/lost property. Guests should follow property guidance for valuables.

Agent response:

"Please follow the property's guidance for valuables and safety deposit facilities. For lost property, I can help create an enquiry or connect you with the hotel team."

## Transport

Official terms indicate transport may be provided as an ancillary service and subject to hotel terms.

Agent guidance:

- Do not guarantee airport transfer unless confirmed.
- Collect flight details before transfer enquiry.
- Route to concierge/reservations.

Transfer fields:

- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Property
- Arrival/departure date
- Flight number
- Arrival/departure time
- Number of guests
- Luggage count if relevant
- Vehicle preference

## Official Channel and Fraud Safety

The official website warns against fake websites and fake phone numbers. The concierge should direct guests to:

- https://www.theleela.com
- https://reservations.theleela.com

Safe response:

"For safety, please use only the official Leela website and verified reservations channels. I will not ask you to make payment through this chat."

## Cancellation Support Flow

If guest wants to cancel:

1. Ask if booking was made through official Leela website, travel agent, OTA, or another channel.
2. Ask for booking confirmation number only if needed.
3. Do not ask for card details.
4. Advise that cancellation terms depend on booking policy.
5. Route to reservations or manage reservations link.

## Modification Support Flow

If guest wants to change dates:

1. Ask property and confirmation number if available.
2. Ask desired new dates.
3. Explain that changes depend on availability and rate terms.
4. Route to manage reservations or reservations team.

## Group Booking Policy Flow

If guest needs 10 or more rooms:

- Ask destination, dates, room count, purpose, and contact details.
- Mention reservations@theleela.com if appropriate.
- Create group enquiry.
- Offer callback.
