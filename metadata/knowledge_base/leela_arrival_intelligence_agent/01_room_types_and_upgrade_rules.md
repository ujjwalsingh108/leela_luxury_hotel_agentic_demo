# The Leela Demo Knowledge Base
# Room Types and Upgrade Rules

Document purpose: Ground the agent on room categories, upgrade logic, and approval rules for the Royal Arrival Intelligence demo.

Data classification: Synthetic demo content. Do not treat as official The Leela commercial policy.

## Room Categories

### Premier Room
- Entry luxury room category.
- Best for solo business travelers, short-stay leisure guests, and corporate delegates.
- Typical features: king or twin bed, work desk, marble bathroom, city or garden view.
- Demo positioning: Comfortable luxury base category.

### Conservatory Premier Room
- Enhanced room category with more natural light and a refined sitting area.
- Best for repeat leisure guests and guests who value quiet time in-room.
- Demo positioning: Tasteful upgrade for Gold and Platinum guests when occupancy permits.

### Royal Club Room
- Premium category with club-style benefits.
- Best for business travelers, loyalty members, and guests with meetings on property.
- Typical benefits in demo: priority check-in, evening refreshments, enhanced workspace.
- Demo positioning: Strong upgrade recommendation for elite business guests.

### Lake-View Room
- Scenic category used in the demo for Udaipur, Bengaluru, or resort-style properties.
- Best for honeymoon, anniversary, leisure, and first-time international guests.
- Demo positioning: High emotional impact upgrade for occasions.

### Royal Suite
- Luxury suite with separate living area.
- Best for VIPs, long-stay guests, families, and high-value loyalty members.
- Demo positioning: Approval-gated upgrade for Diamond or Titanium guests.

### Maharaja Suite
- Ultra-premium suite category.
- Best for dignitaries, celebrities, wedding principals, and top-tier lifetime-value guests.
- Demo positioning: Never auto-offer. Always requires General Manager or Rooms Director approval.

### Presidential Suite
- Highest demo category.
- Best for state guests, ultra-high-value guests, ownership guests, and confidential VIPs.
- Demo positioning: Manual-only. Agent may flag eligibility but must not promise availability.

## Upgrade Eligibility Rules

The agent may recommend an upgrade only when all of the following are true:
- Booking status is Confirmed or Checked In.
- Guest has a high-value signal, such as VIP flag, Platinum or above loyalty tier, special occasion, high lifetime spend, or service recovery need.
- Occupancy and room availability support an upgrade.
- The recommendation is positioned as "subject to approval and availability."

## Approval Matrix

### Agent may recommend without approval
- Premier Room to Conservatory Premier Room.
- Premier Room to Royal Club Room for Gold, Platinum, Titanium, or Diamond guests.
- Lake-View Room suggestion for honeymoon or anniversary guests, subject to availability.

### Front Office approval required
- Royal Club Room to Royal Suite.
- Any upgrade for OTA bookings where rate parity or channel policy may apply.
- Any upgrade requested before arrival but not paid.

### Revenue Manager approval required
- Complimentary upgrade during high-demand or event dates.
- Upgrade when forecast occupancy is above 75 percent.
- Upgrade involving meaningful revenue displacement.

### General Manager approval required
- Maharaja Suite or Presidential Suite.
- Celebrity, dignitary, ownership, or highly confidential VIP.
- Any compensation-linked upgrade after a serious complaint.

## Upgrade Recommendation Language

Use:
"Mr. Sharma appears eligible for a Lake-View Suite consideration, subject to Front Office approval and live availability."

Do not use:
"Mr. Sharma has been upgraded."

Use:
"This is a tasteful occasion-led upgrade opportunity."

Do not use:
"We should upsell aggressively."

## Upgrade Ranking Logic

Rank upgrade candidates in this order:
1. Safety or service recovery need.
2. VIP or Diamond guest.
3. Special occasion such as honeymoon, anniversary, milestone birthday, or family celebration.
4. High lifetime spend or frequent repeat stay.
5. Direct booking or GHA DISCOVERY booking.
6. First-time international guest where upgrade may improve brand memory.

## Red Flags

Do not recommend upgrades when:
- The guest has an unresolved complaint that requires human recovery strategy.
- The guest has requested privacy or minimal contact.
- The property is under high compression and Revenue has not approved inventory use.
- The booking is cancelled, no-show, or checked out.
